1. Introduction

Boardzen is an educational Android application that helps students access study materials and learning tools, including content for CBSE and UP Board classes. The app is associated with the brand DNS Creation and developed by Saavej Choudhary.

This policy describes information that may be processed when you use Boardzen. Actual processing depends on the features you use, the permissions you grant, and current service configuration.

Important: This policy is drafted from the project details available, not a direct inspection of the complete source code or live SDK configuration. Before publication, verify it against the current app build, Google Play Data safety form, and provider settings.

2. Information handled by Boardzen

Depending on the features used, Boardzen may process:

  • Account information: phone number, profile name, and other fields you submit when using an account.
  • Learning information: selected class, subjects, lesson or paper references, and interactions needed to provide study features.
  • Submitted content: text questions, images of questions, chat messages, group details, requests, and feedback you choose to submit.
  • Technical information: session, diagnostic, and service information needed to operate the app and protect reliability, depending on implementation and provider configuration.
  • Advertising information: where ads are enabled, AdMob and its partners may process ad-related identifiers and interaction or diagnostic information as described in their disclosures.

3. Accounts and profiles

Boardzen's documented login flow uses a phone number and password. The app and backend may process submitted account and profile information to authenticate you, maintain your profile, and associate content with your account. Confirm the live release's exact fields before publishing.

Keep your password private and avoid putting sensitive information in profile fields or messages.

4. Educational content and offline downloads

Boardzen provides learning materials such as notes, PDFs, eBooks, and Previous Year Papers where available. Opening or downloading a document may access it from its configured source.

Offline downloads may store a local copy on your device for use without an internet connection. Removing a local download does not necessarily delete the original server-side document. Files may be removed if you clear app data or uninstall the app, depending on the storage implementation.

5. AI Tutor

The AI Tutor accepts text questions and, where enabled, images of questions. Submitted content may be sent to Boardzen's backend and an AI provider to generate an answer. The project has been described as using a Gemini model through a Supabase Edge Function; confirm the currently deployed model and provider settings before publication.

Do not submit passwords, identity documents, financial information, or other sensitive personal data. AI answers can be incorrect and should be checked against trusted learning materials. Provider retention and training settings should be reviewed against the provider's current terms.

6. Community, study groups, and messages

If you use community or group features, submitted messages, group details, join requests, and profile information may be stored and shown to relevant group members to provide those features. Other participants may copy or capture content outside Boardzen, so share thoughtfully.

OneSignal is used for app-wide push notifications and announcements, such as updates or new learning content. Do not assume push announcements are private one-to-one chat delivery.

Ad personalization depends on the live SDK configuration, consent requirements, and device or Google settings.

9. Device permissions

Permissions depend on the shipped Android build and features used. Image-question submission may use a system photo picker or camera if camera capture is implemented; push notifications may require notification permission; file download and opening may use app storage or file access. Verify the actual Android manifest and runtime requests before publication.

This policy does not claim that Boardzen accesses contacts, microphone, precise location, or other device data unless the current app actually requests and uses that access.

10. How information is used

  • Provide account sign-in and profile features.
  • Display learning materials and support offline access.
  • Process AI Tutor questions and return responses.
  • Operate community, group, and messaging features.
  • Send app announcements and notifications.
  • Display and measure advertising where enabled.
  • Maintain security, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

11. How information may be shared

Information may be processed by providers that operate features you use, including backend/storage, AI processing, push notifications, and advertising. Content you post in a group or send to another user may be visible to those recipients. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

Boardzen does not sell personal information to advertisers as a separate business practice. Advertising providers may process information under their own policies to deliver advertising services.

12. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained as needed to provide features, maintain service records, meet operational needs, or comply with legal obligations. Specific retention periods depend on the data type and backend/provider configuration; no universal deletion period is promised here.

Local downloads may remain on your device until deleted, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. To ask about your account or request deletion, email savejch7464@gmail.com. Do not send your password. Account ownership may need to be verified.

13. Data security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information, such as secure connections and restricted backend access. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you suspect unauthorized account access, contact the developer.

14. Students and children's privacy

Boardzen is an educational app and may be used by school students. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in AI questions, profiles, or group chats. Parents or guardians with privacy questions about a student's use may contact the developer.

This policy does not claim certification under a particular children's privacy law or that parental consent is collected in-app. The developer must verify applicable age requirements, consent flows, and Google Play Families and advertising requirements before publication.

15. Your choices and privacy rights

You can control what you submit, adjust Android notification or app permissions, manage local downloads, and use account controls available in the app. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other handling of personal information. Contact the developer to make a request.

16. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when Boardzen features, data practices, providers, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at the policy URL with an updated date. Review it periodically.
